Computational Optics and Displays Engineering

We co-design optical systems and algorithms to sense, represent, and infer from high-dimensional visual signals such as videos, hyperspectral images, and lightfields.

Meet the team Read our papers Join us

Research

We work at the intersection of optics, metamaterials, machine learning, and signal processing, co-designing imaging systems in which the camera and the algorithms behind it are treated as a single problem — from the physics of light to the priors of deep networks.
